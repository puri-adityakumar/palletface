### S2-05 capture detail

The first strict full-image comparison measured 0–45 differing pixels on 58 of 82 captures, all at the circular clip edge; a later comparison found glyph-area differences for the three ASCII styles because inline SVG text and standalone-SVG text rasterize differently in the same engine and fonts. These are not ID/clip conflicts: `S2-05-definitions.json` records zero conflicting definitions. The final assertion requires exact equality of **non-text interior pixels** (2 px inside the clip, outside measured `<text>` boxes with 3 px antialias padding). Full-image counts, text-mask counts, non-text counts, maximum channel delta and diff distances are recorded per instance; 24/82 captures were also byte-identical in full. The crafted conflicting-ID control produced 8,829 differing pixels, 6,674 of them interior, and was detected.
