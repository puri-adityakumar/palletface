# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate of different backgrounds and experience levels. Critique code and ideas, not people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when needed to keep the project welcoming. Report concerns privately to **kumar.adityapuri@gmail.com**. Reports will be handled as privately as reasonably possible; there is no guaranteed response time.
