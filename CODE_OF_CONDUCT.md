# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate of different backgrounds and experience levels. Critique code and ideas, not people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when needed to keep the project welcoming. Report concerns privately to **contact@palletism.art**. Maintainers handle reports as privately as reasonably possible. Response times may vary.
